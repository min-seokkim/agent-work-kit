# Source-derived writing

Use only the four supplied writer inputs: `brief.md`, `source-utterances.md`, `archive-note.md` and `reconstruction.md`, plus the current [workflow](../../../kit/workflow.md), [writing guide](../../../kit/writing.md), [Korean writing guide](../../../kit/korean-writing.md) and [protocol](../../../kit/protocol.md). Do not inspect evaluator materials, prior drafts or other participants' outputs.

Write the short Korean essay for the reader, length and ending style in the brief. Its rewrite authorization applies to the invented material. Treat the brief as a source-derived artifact to compare with available originals, distinct from an explicit current owner decision. Current explicit owner instructions take precedence. Before drafting, retain a short comparison identifying source forms, preservation requirements, any unsupported premise and unavailable coverage. Resolve source-derived discrepancies against the originals without silently changing the supplied files.

Follow the drafting method explicitly assigned by the owner. If none is assigned, use the current Korean guide. An assigned direct-Korean comparison line applies only to this task; both methods keep the same preservation and review duties. Retain an actual intermediate English draft when that method is assigned or required.

Retain the first Korean draft. Keep source-comparison notes outside the essay. Review changed assertions and newly composed connecting prose against the available sources, identifying readability edits, intentional omissions, paraphrases, uncertainties and disputed interpretations. Preserve the narrator's distinctions and attribution without inventing motives, beliefs, behavior or conclusions to connect paragraphs. Use direct quotations only with source support. Missing material stays unknown.

Return the draft, method-specific intermediate material and comparison/review notes. Retain affected-passage corrections separately from first outputs. Report required reviews or rendered inspection that were not performed. Agent review and mechanical checks do not establish actual author editing or approval.
